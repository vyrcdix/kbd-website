You don’t have to know what to say

Most people write two lines, which is plenty, and we can work the rest out together. There’s no intake form here and nothing to explain in advance.

  • hello@kristinadixon.ca
  • 250 000 0000 (leave a message; I return calls between sessions)
  • Penticton, BC, and online across British Columbia

Send me a message

Three fields, one of them required. Nothing about funding, diagnosis, or history is asked before we’ve spoken.

In person

A child-sized room in Penticton with a sand tray, art materials, and floor space. I’ll send the address and parking details with your first booking.

Online

Secure video through Jane, anywhere in BC, which works well for parent sessions and older children.

If it’s urgent

This inbox isn’t monitored after hours. In a crisis, please call 9-8-8 (the suicide crisis helpline), the Kids Help Phone at 1-800-668-6868, or 9-1-1.

I live and work on the unceded, ancestral, and traditional territory of the Sqilx̌ʷ/Syilx Okanagan people. I hold gratitude for the land, and for the children and families who have shared their stories with me here.
